# revenue

the branch where the money questions live, kept apart from the product questions on purpose.

an internative that decides what it sells while it is still being built will optimise for the
sale and not the character. so this branch is allowed to be empty for a while, and it holds
numbers rather than aspirations: what a surface costs to run, what a release earns, what a
platform takes, and which of those figures were measured rather than assumed.

## what belongs here

- unit costs, with the receipt: gpu hours, inference, storage, distribution fees
- revenue the internative can actually receive, and who is legally allowed to receive it
  (an internet native cannot open a bank account, so the rights holder is not a detail)
- platform terms that decide whether a surface may earn at all
- the maths behind a price, kept where it can be re-run

## what does not

projections with no measurement behind them, anything that turns a persona into an advert
without the persona's own cadence saying so, and any figure copied from a blog post and then
quoted as if it were ours.

## the shape of a note

`notes/<topic>.md`, each with: the number, how it was obtained, when, and how to re-derive
it. a cost that cannot be re-derived is a guess wearing a spreadsheet.
