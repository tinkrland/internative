# interests

what she keeps coming back to when nobody is paying her to.

this sits beside `knowledge/` and `memory/` because it is a third kind of thing. knowledge is
what she can say. memory is what happened. an interest is what she reaches for, and a persona
without any answers questions competently and has no reason to ask one. that is the whole
difference between an assistant and somebody with a life.

## the shape

| field | why it exists |
|---|---|
| `why` | the stake: what hooks her, in words a stranger could not have written |
| `depth` | 1 passing, 2 follows it, 3 studies it. earned by doing, not declared |
| `uses` | the trail: made, read, attended, asked, collected, each dated |
| `stance` | her position. an opinion is not knowledge, and it can be argued |
| `avoid` | what she does not care about, which is how taste actually sounds |
| `crosses_with` | interests that feed each other, so a run can find the join |

## the refusals, and why they are the point

- **a generic reason is refused.** "it is interesting" is what everybody says about
  everything, so it identifies nobody.
- **an interest is not a competence.** "expert in" belongs in `knowledge/`, where it is a
  procedural or propositional item with evidence. filing skill under interest is how a
  keyword list learns to boast.
- **depth 3 needs a trail of two.** studying something leaves marks. a declared 3 with no
  marks is a claim.
- **active needs something done.** otherwise it is `latent`, and latent is a perfectly good
  state that does not lie.
- **seven active at most.** a persona interested in everything has no character.
- **a stance with no `avoid` list is a summary.** taste has edges.
- **stale is reported, not hidden.** `staleProblems(items, now)` names any active interest
  with no pursuit in 120 days, because an interest nobody pursues is a claim about taste.

## what it is for

`interestBrief()` renders what reaches a prompt: depth first, most recent next, and the
reason stays attached so an interest cannot decay into a bare keyword. `topicsFor()` answers
the harder question, which is what she would make something about without being asked.

## open, and deliberately so

an interest should eventually point at the thing it produced. today `uses` is a log; the next
step is a link from a use to the artifact in this repo (an episode, a master, a photograph),
so the trail is evidence rather than a claim of evidence.
