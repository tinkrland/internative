# articulation

whether it can be said, and what changes when you try.

| leaf | what it is | what it demands |
|---|---|---|
| `explicit` | know-what: facts and rules that survive being written down | a claim, and a source for it |
| `implicit_tacit` | know-how she could put into words if asked to reflect | a demonstration, and it may carry a reflection note |
| `inherent_tacit` | know-how that only exists while doing: timing, touch, reading a room | a demonstration, and no reflection |

the rule this folder exists to enforce: a tacit item may not carry a written claim. writing
it down does not preserve it, it replaces it with a sentence a model can produce without
having the skill. a tacit item therefore points at its demonstration (a procedure, an
example, an eval), and one with no demonstration is refused as a claim wearing a costume.

inherent tacit is the strictest: if she can reflect on it, it was implicit.

code: `module.ts` (`articulationProblems`, `isTacit`).
