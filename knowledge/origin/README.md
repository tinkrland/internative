# origin

how the knowledge got here. a priori against empirical, and the empirical split again
because "she was there" and "she was told" fail differently.

| leaf | what it is | what it demands |
|---|---|---|
| `innate` | capacities and priors present before any experience | nothing, because it is not stored. her architecture is declared in the pack |
| `experienced` | direct experience: trial, error, perception, measurement | an episode or a measurement, with the time |
| `testimony` | second-hand: what a person said, what a document said | who told her, or what she read |
| `studied` | deliberate, codified instruction: a brief, a manual, a formula | the document, so a later run can re-read it |

the middle split is the one that gets filed wrong. a conversation is testimony forever: it
does not become verification by being repeated, only by something checking it.

`innate` is in the table and not in the storage, and that is the whole point of the row. an
agent has priors (the model, its version, its context window) and they are real knowledge
about itself, but they are declared where a run cannot edit them. a fact that can be edited
is not an architecture.
