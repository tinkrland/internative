# function

what the knowledge is for. four answers, and the test for each is different.

| leaf | the question it answers | the shape it takes |
|---|---|---|
| `propositional` | is it true | a claim |
| `procedural` | can she do it | a demonstrated procedure, example or eval |
| `explanatory` | why does it happen | a claim plus the phenomenon it explains |
| `acquaintance` | has she met it | an entity, and no claim about it |

acquaintance is the one a machine taxonomy forgets, and its absence is why personas claim
intimacy with people they have exchanged three sentences with. knowing Ana is not a
proposition about Ana, so an acquaintance item that carries a claim is refused: the claims
belong in propositional items with their own sources, and some of them will be wrong.

explanatory is the only leaf that transfers to a new case, which is why an explanation that
cannot be stated is refused. procedural is where a demonstration is mandatory.

what she can REACH is not here. tool-calling is `access/`: knowing how to change a tyre is
knowledge, having the keys to the van is access.
