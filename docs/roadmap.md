# roadmap

direction, in order, with the honest caveat that an order is not a promise.

## the head, now

done, and the reason the rest is possible: a persona is a manifest, the
registers are configuration, the brief is generated, the voice mapping is one
file, the anchors and the memory scope are declared per surface, and publishing
is a permission rather than an assumption.

## next

1. **the port lands.** the seven surface branches carry the proven rails from
   the prixie work (meeting joins, the obs bridge, the media pipeline, the
   plate and set system) adapted to the head, so nothing is rewritten to prove
   a point.
2. **the surfaces that are genuinely new**: longform as written video (not a
   republished stream), audio episodes with chapters and a feed, and text
   presence with real per network manners.
3. **youtube live**, explicitly, because twitch and kick already work and
   youtube is the destination that also owns long form and shorts. one surface,
   three destinations with three different shapes.
4. **the cross surface story**: a stream becomes clips becomes a post becomes
   an episode, each one carrying the persona's memory of the source. the
   interesting part is not the repurposing, it is that the persona remembers
   making it.

## later

- a persona registry outside the repo (manifests fetched, not committed) for
  running many personas from one deployment.
- per pair memory overrides (this person may hear about that meeting).
- a calendar the persona runs itself: the docket idea from the proxy work,
  applied to publishing slots as well as meetings.
- a review surface: one page where the owner sees every artifact waiting on
  approval, across all seven surfaces, instead of seven queues.

## deliberately out of scope

- generating faces or voices that are not the persona's own.
- engagement farming, follow loops, or anything that fakes a human audience.
- posting as a real person, a client, or anyone the persona is not.
