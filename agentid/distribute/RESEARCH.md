# what their own pages say

read on 9 november 2026, without creating an account anywhere. each section is what
the vendor publishes, not what an affiliate blog claims. the flow files follow these
pages; where a page says nothing, the flow says nothing and reads the uploader at
run time instead.

## routenote

the upload guide names every page of the consumer flow: release data, album details,
audio, artwork, manage stores, localisation, finalise.

- UPC left blank is generated free, and a code from a previous distributor goes there
- a sales start date has to be at least two weeks away
- artist page is either selected from spotify or created during the first release
- writers and lyricists are named separately, and both are required when there are lyrics
- composition copyright (C line) and sound recording copyright (P line) are separate fields
- the label name cannot be none, unsigned, indie, N/A or independent, or it is rejected
- ISRC fills automatically, and a previous code goes in on a move
- the artist and label agreement has to be accepted or nothing can be submitted
- then Distribute Free or Distribute Premium, then moderation may ask for fixes

## indiefy

the flow is inverted: the upload form is public and the account is created at the end.

- audio: wav, mp3, flac, aac, up to 200 MB per file
- release details asked before signup: title, artist name, main genre
- signup asks first name, last name, email, password, terms
- a cover is auto generated from the title and can be swapped before submission
- live in 5 to 7 business days on a paid plan, 15 to 30 on free
- free accounts withdraw 90 days after the request, paid 30
- non exclusive, and metadata and stores stay editable until the release is sent

## freecords

the uploader and the distributor are two different products.

- register from the for artists page and tick are you an artist, or the account is a listener
- audio: mp3 320 kbps, wav, flac, up to 100 MB per file
- cover art: jpg or png, up to 10 MB, minimum 600 square, square recommended
- upload lives behind Upload Music on the app home
- distribution rides The Club, which has its own terms and its own FAQ
- free, no commission, unlimited releases, 100 plus platforms, covers ride a separate product

## jumpstr

the landing page answers money and not delivery.

- free, unlimited uploads, keep 100 percent
- custom label name shown on all stores
- stores listed: spotify, apple, amazon, tidal, tiktok, youtube, deezer and others
- no format list, no artwork floor, no help centre: support is the widget on every page
- removal is a support request, and switching distributors means removing and
  re-uploading with the original ISRC
- moderation speed is an artist testimonial, not a published service level

## spacemedia

the how it works list is five steps and they are all on the landing page.

- register, choose a plan, create a release and upload, their team reviews quality,
  then payouts
- free is zero upfront with the standard royalties, unlimited releases, 250 plus platforms
- withdrawals from 25 euros, reported as paid within 24 hours
- upload requirements are not published outside the uploader

## soundon

the one that does not map cleanly.

- sign in happens through a tiktok account, so the tiktok login is the first gate
- the only real signup page their site exposes is the login entry point
- their public material is a forum of search shaped articles rather than a help centre:
  metadata lists and a 1600 square artwork figure appear there, page field sets do not
- so this flow is marked unverified, and the dashboard stays unmapped until someone
  reads the real upload page behind the tiktok sign in

## the live pages, 9 november 2026

read in a real browser, no account created anywhere. these four corrected the map:

**routenote signup** asks for a username, an email, a password typed twice, a security
question and its answer, an optional referral code, a "stay up to date" box, and the
terms box. there is no date of birth on the page, so the stop moved from dob to terms.
the security answer is a credential and is generated per host, never reused.

**soundon signup** has no password at all. it has a with email tab, a with phone tab,
a registration country sentence, a send code button, a code field, a marketing emails
box, a box that accepts the terms and confirms 18 plus, then create an account, with
continue with tiktok and continue with google as equal doors. the account is the
mailbox, and both social doors stop at an existing account because that account is the
owner's.

**jumpstr register** asks for a name, an email, a password typed twice, and has no
terms checkbox: a sentence states that registering means they may contact you about
their products. so the stop is that sentence, read by the owner before the button.

**indiefy** confirms the inverted order live: the release form is public and asks for
the file, a title, an artist and a genre, with the account created after.

freecords carries a cookie banner on every page (i want to choose, accept all, accept
selection), which is the prixie rule again: never accept all.
